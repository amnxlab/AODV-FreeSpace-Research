About GAODV

                    

                        GAODV (Geometric and Energy-Aware AODV) is an enhanced version of the AODV routing protocol that incorporates energy awareness and geometric considerations for improved performance in Mobile Ad-hoc Networks (MANETs).
                    

                    
                    
Key Features

                    
    
                        
  • Energy-Aware Routing: Selects routes based on node energy levels
    • 
                        
  • Route Evaluation Factor (REF): Multi-metric path selection
    • 
                        
  • Energy Threshold: Prevents overuse of low-energy nodes
    • 
                        
  • Battery Life Prediction: Real-time energy consumption tracking
    •
